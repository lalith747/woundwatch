# Phase-1 Submission Copy

## Idea title

WoundWatch AI

## Description

WoundWatch AI is a phone-first recovery companion for people monitoring minor wounds over time. Users capture a consistent image, log pain and symptoms by voice or touch, and receive an easy-to-understand recovery snapshot and timeline. The prototype focuses on observable change rather than diagnosis: it combines longitudinal visual signals with user-reported symptoms and provides safety prompts when concerning changes are reported. A CareGuide explains the signals in simple language and explicitly avoids identifying pathogens, diagnosing infection, or prescribing treatment.

## Prior builds & hackathons

Built and shipped AI/ML prototypes across hackathons and academic projects, including KiranaMitra (AI inventory assistant), SAMVED Smart Water Pressure Management, UrbanFlow road-defect monitoring, CivicMind AI, and RecoverAI. Experience spans Python, FastAPI, React/TypeScript, Flutter, computer vision, Generative AI, Firebase/Supabase and cloud deployment.

## What makes you and your team stand out?

Our three-person team combines AI/ML product engineering, microbiology/health-domain understanding, and cloud/backend experience. The domain member helps define safe, meaningful observations and guardrails; the AI member builds the vision and interaction layer; and the cloud engineer makes the system deployable and scalable. Instead of building another generic health chatbot, we are building a focused phone-first workflow: camera → symptoms/voice → longitudinal signal → safety guidance.
